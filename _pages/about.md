I am a postdoctoral researcher affiliated with the [Data Science Lab](https://dsl.mit.edu/students/) at MIT, and the [Mitch Daniels School of Business](https://business.purdue.edu) at Purdue University, both under the supervision of [Prof. David Simchi-Levi](https://cee.mit.edu/people_individual/david-simchi-levi/). I earned my Ph.D. degree of Operations Research from the [Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/industrial/) at Northwestern University, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Seyed Iravani](http://users.iems.northwestern.edu/~iravani/index.html) and [Prof. Ohad Perry](https://people.smu.edu/operry/homepage/). Prior to Northwestern, I received my bachelor’s degree in Engineering Mechanics at Peking University (ranking top 1 in the major), where I was advised by [Prof. Leyuan Shi](https://directory.engr.wisc.edu/ie/Faculty/Shi_Leyuan/).  

My research centers on the performance analysis, design, and control of stochastic systems through applying and developing tools in applied probability, queueing theory, and optimization. A key focus of my work is understanding the value of ***redundancy***, ***flexibility***, and their ***interplay***---with both their limited ("a little") and full versions---in improving (or potentially harming) the efficiency of online service operations, including search engines, cloud computing and storage systems, and LLM serving platforms.
